Overview


My research focuses on understanding how aquatic ecosystems function, how ecological interactions shape ecosystem processes, and how environmental change influences biodiversity and ecosystem resilience.

As a freshwater and stream ecologist, I investigate the connections between organismal behavior, species interactions, food-web dynamics, and ecosystem functioning. My work integrates field observations, experiments, and quantitative approaches to explore how energy flows through aquatic ecosystems and how ecological processes respond to changing environmental conditions.

I am particularly interested in understanding how natural variability, extreme events, and anthropogenic disturbances influence aquatic communities and ecosystem processes across different environmental contexts, including tropical and temperate freshwater systems.

Ultimately, my research aims to advance ecological understanding while contributing to freshwater biodiversity conservation and the sustainable management of aquatic resources.
