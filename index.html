Overview


My research investigates how ecological interactions organize aquatic ecosystems and shape their functioning across spatial and temporal scales.

I am particularly interested in how organisms interact with one another and their environment, and how these interactions influence biodiversity, food-web structure, energy flow, and ecosystem processes. By integrating ecological theory, field experiments, and quantitative approaches, I seek to connect individual-level mechanisms with broader patterns of ecosystem organization.

A central goal of my research is to understand how variation in environmental conditions and ecological interactions influences the stability, resilience, and functioning of freshwater ecosystems.

Through this work, I aim to develop a mechanistic understanding of aquatic ecology that can inform biodiversity conservation and the management of freshwater resources.
